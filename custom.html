The Source

Every product in our catalogue started as a custom formulation. We produce our amino acid base in-house through protein hydrolysis — which means the amino acid profile itself is a variable we control, not a fixed input we purchase. The free-L amino acid spectrum, nitrogen content, and molecular weight distribution can all be tuned to the target crop and application method before any additional ingredient is introduced.

On top of this foundation, we compound whatever the formulation requires — seaweed extracts, polysaccharides, vitamins, macro and trace elements, microorganisms, or silicon. The result is a finished biostimulant built around a specific agronomic objective, not a generic blend relabelled for your market.

How Custom Formulation Works

From Brief to Batch

01
Briefing
Tell us your target crop, application window, regulatory market, and any specific agronomic objective — stress tolerance, fruit set, quality enhancement, or reduced input programmes.
→
02
Formulation Design
We design the amino acid base composition and select add-ons. Where phytohormone content, micronutrient balance, or organic certification matters, we flag those constraints early.
→
03
Small Batch Trial
A pilot batch is produced for field or greenhouse evaluation. We can support with trial design and share standard measurement protocols aligned with our existing trial methodology.
→
04
Scale-Up
Once trial data confirms the formulation, we move to production scale. We handle SDS documentation, CLP labelling requirements, and can produce under private label if needed.
